# The Thinking Gym

> **Just as the body weakens without exercise, so does the mind.**

The Thinking Gym is an open-source collection of mental exercises designed to improve reasoning, curiosity, prediction, creativity, communication, skepticism, judgment, and confidence calibration.

Its purpose is not to teach people **what** to think. Its purpose is to help people practice **how** to think.

## Core principle

> **The goal is not to become right. The goal is to become harder to fool.**

That includes being harder to fool by other people, artificial intelligence, institutions, media, emotion, habit, and one's own previous conclusions.

## How it works

The project runs through named thinking exercises. Each completed session leaves behind a permanent artifact recording:

- the question or challenge;
- the participant's starting position;
- the strongest opposing argument or alternative model;
- the reasoning process and important discoveries;
- confidence before and after;
- unresolved questions; and
- one closing sentence answering: **What changed?**

The repository is both a training program and a longitudinal record of how reasoning develops over time.

## Development phases

### Phase I — Foundation

Build and complete the initial exercise cycle. Learn the mechanics of honest inquiry, strong counterargument, systems analysis, forecasting, and reflective judgment.

### Phase II — Calibration

Compare predictions with outcomes, track recurring blind spots, measure confidence accuracy, and identify which cognitive skills require more deliberate practice.

### Phase III — Adaptive Training

Generate new exercises based on observed weaknesses. The Gym should train what needs training rather than repeating a comfortable routine indefinitely.

## The Gym does not protect the ego

No idea receives immunity because it belongs to the participant, the facilitator, an expert, a famous person, a tradition, or a majority.

Every idea may be questioned. Every idea must also be represented fairly before it is tested.

The objective is not destruction. It is accurate understanding under pressure.

## Knowledge and reality

The Gym distinguishes between a claim being unsupported by currently available evidence and a claim being impossible. Human and AI evaluators both operate with incomplete information.

See the [Principles Index](principles/INDEX.md), including:

## Start a session

1. Choose an exercise.
2. Copy [`templates/session-template.md`](templates/session-template.md) into `sessions/`.
3. Name it with a sequential identifier, such as `0001-convince-me.md`.
4. Record the starting belief, prediction, model, or problem before substantial discussion begins.
5. Complete the exercise without hiding uncertainty or protecting conclusions.
6. End with **What changed?**
7. Commit the artifact even when the result is inconclusive.

## Preservation rule

Conversation is the workshop. The repository is the source of truth.

A durable principle, framework, exercise improvement, prediction, or methodological rule is not considered preserved until it has been committed. When one appears during a session, capture it before the discussion moves far enough for it to be lost.
