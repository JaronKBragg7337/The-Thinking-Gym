# Version 1 — Foundation Cycle

**Started:** July 30, 2026  
**Status:** Active

## Objective

Establish the first repeatable cycle of exercises and complete one permanent session artifact for each exercise.

## Cycle

1. Convince Me
2. Rabbit Hole
3. Invent a Civilization
4. Break the Invention
5. Future Headlines
6. Impossible Problems
7. The AI Interview

**Bonus:** Random Word

## Graduation criteria

Version 1 is complete when:

- all seven foundation exercises have been completed at least once;
- every session has a permanent artifact;
- confidence before and after has been recorded where applicable;
- each session ends with “What changed?”;
- recurring strengths and weaknesses have been reviewed; and
- Version 2 introduces exercises targeted at the patterns observed during Version 1.

## Planned evolution

Version 2 should not merely repeat the original cycle. Candidate additions include:

- steelman an opponent;
- detect hidden assumptions;
- design an experiment to test a belief;
- find three ways a conclusion could be wrong;
- explain an expert concept to a child;
- predict unintended consequences;
- build two fundamentally different solutions; and
- reverse-engineer why something succeeded.

Final selection should be based on evidence gathered from completed sessions rather than novelty alone.
