# Invent a Civilization

## Purpose

Build a coherent civilization from one altered rule or initial condition. The exercise trains systems thinking by tracing consequences across biology, culture, economics, infrastructure, governance, conflict, technology, and daily life.

## Core method

Choose one foundational change, such as weaker gravity, perfect dream recall, scarce sunlight, or nonverbal communication. Derive consequences rather than decorating a familiar society with the premise.

## Rules

1. State the altered rule precisely.
2. Identify immediate physical or behavioral consequences.
3. Trace second- and third-order effects across multiple systems.
4. Include tradeoffs, inequality, adaptation, failure, and unintended consequences.
5. Test whether institutions and technologies remain internally consistent.
6. Record at least one feature that initially seemed beneficial but became costly, and one apparent disadvantage that produced an advantage.

## Completion standard

The artifact describes a civilization whose major systems follow plausibly from the starting condition and identifies where the model remains uncertain.
