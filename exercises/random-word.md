# Random Word

## Purpose

Use one arbitrary word as the seed for unrestricted but disciplined exploration. The exercise trains associative thinking, analogy, conceptual transfer, and the ability to uncover depth in ordinary subjects.

## Core method

Choose a word without optimizing for importance. Explore its physical properties, history, uses, metaphors, systems, consequences, and unexpected connections. Follow promising branches while preserving a traceable path.

## Rules

1. Use the selected word without replacing it with an easier topic.
2. Begin with direct observations before moving into analogy or speculation.
3. Make each conceptual jump explicit.
4. Explore at least three distinct domains.
5. Identify one connection that initially appears absurd but becomes defensible.
6. End with a question that could not have been predicted from the starting word.

## Completion standard

The artifact shows a coherent path from an arbitrary seed to a nontrivial insight or question.
