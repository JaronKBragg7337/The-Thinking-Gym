# Rabbit Hole

## Purpose

Follow one subject through successive layers of explanation until the discussion reaches first principles, hidden dependencies, or a meaningful boundary of current knowledge.

## Core method

Begin with a concrete question. Repeatedly ask what causes, supports, constrains, or explains the current answer. Each layer must connect logically to the previous one.

## Rules

1. Start with one defined topic or question.
2. Separate established knowledge from inference and speculation.
3. Do not confuse greater complexity with greater depth.
4. Record each major layer of the inquiry.
5. Stop only at a justified boundary: first principles, insufficient evidence, unresolved research, or diminishing relevance.
6. End with the deepest useful insight and the strongest unanswered question.

## Completion standard

The final artifact shows the path from the starting question to deeper mechanisms and identifies where explanation currently ends.
