# The AI Interview

## Purpose

Explore judgment through difficult decisions involving leadership, institutions, companies, governments, expeditions, colonies, emergencies, or other systems with competing objectives.

The artificial intelligence is not treated as an oracle. Its role is to expose assumptions, produce alternative policies, and explain tradeoffs for examination.

## Core method

Place the decision-maker inside a defined scenario. Ask what they would do, why, what they would sacrifice, which risks they would accept, and how they would respond when the first plan fails.

## Rules

1. Define the role, authority, constraints, stakeholders, and time horizon.
2. Identify objectives that conflict rather than pretending every value can be maximized simultaneously.
3. Require a decision, not only abstract commentary.
4. Trace who benefits, who bears costs, and who lacks representation.
5. Examine incentives, enforcement, failure, reversibility, and abuse of power.
6. Compare at least one alternative course of action.
7. Distinguish moral preference from operational consequence.
8. End with the principle or tradeoff that governed the final judgment.

## Completion standard

The artifact records the scenario, options, chosen action, rejected alternatives, affected stakeholders, failure contingencies, and the reasoning principle that determined the decision.
