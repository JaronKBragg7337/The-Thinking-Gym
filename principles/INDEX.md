# Principles Index

The principles define how Thinking Gym sessions should handle evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, imagination, and revision.

## Core documents

- [Philosophy](philosophy.md) — The project's foundational commitments and long-term objective.
- [Model Thinking](model-thinking.md) — How to build, test, revise, and replace explanatory models without suppressing curiosity.
- [Knowledge Boundaries](knowledge-boundaries.md) — How to separate the limits of available knowledge from claims about the limits of reality.

## Repository rule

A principle discovered during a session is not considered preserved until it is committed to this repository.

When a discussion produces a durable principle, framework, exercise improvement, prediction, or methodological rule, capture it before continuing far enough for the insight to be lost.
